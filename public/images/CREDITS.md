# Sourced imagery — provenance

Camera photography used in the accent slots. **All CC0 1.0** (public domain
dedication): no attribution is legally required and none is rendered on the
site. Recorded here anyway so the origin of every non-Eitan asset on the site
is traceable when the project is handed over.

CC0 was a hard filter during sourcing. CC BY and CC BY-SA assets are far more
plentiful — Wikimedia Commons has excellent camera photography at 4000–6000px —
but they carry a per-use attribution obligation that would travel with the site
forever, which is the opposite of what this project is built for.

## Resolution ceiling

These arrive at roughly 960–1024px on the long edge, which is StockSnap's CDN
cap, not a processing choice. That is the reason camera imagery is confined to
accent slots: at a 480px display width it is exactly sharp at 2x, and it would
visibly soften if run full-bleed. Eitan's own photographs (up to 3200px) carry
every slot that needs real scale.

Unsplash was not used. It sits behind bot protection that returns 403 to
automated clients, and working around that was not on the table.
